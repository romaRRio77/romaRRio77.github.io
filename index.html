Car Crash Simulator Online

Car Crash Simulator Online is a free car driving and crash physics game. Drive a variety of cars through an open city, perform stunts, crash into obstacles and watch realistic vehicle damage, and play online with other players.

Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Realistic soft-body crash physics and vehicle damage
  • Open city map with ramps, traffic and obstacles
  • Online mode with other players
  • Collection of cars to unlock and drive
  • Cloud saves so your progress is kept across devices

Why the game asks you to sign in with Google

Signing in with your Google account (via Google Play Games) is optional. It is used only to save and restore your game progress in the cloud, so you do not lose your cars and progress when you reinstall the game or switch to another device. The game does not post anything on your behalf and does not access your contacts, files or other Google data.

What this app does not do

Car Crash Simulator Online is a driving game only. It does not generate, edit or share images or videos of people, does not use AI image generation, and does not create any intimate or sexual content.
